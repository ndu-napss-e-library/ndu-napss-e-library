# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for all contributors, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or socioeconomic status.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Helping others learn and grow
- Reporting issues constructively

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate
- Discrimination based on protected characteristics
- Intimidation or threats

---

## Enforcement

### Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by:

1. **Opening a private issue** on GitHub
2. **Contacting project maintainers** directly
3. **Documenting the incident** with details and context

### Response

Project maintainers will:

1. Acknowledge receipt within 48 hours
2. Investigate the complaint
3. Take appropriate action
4. Follow up with the reporter
5. Maintain confidentiality

### Consequences

Unacceptable behavior may result in:

- Warning
- Temporary ban
- Permanent ban
- Action taken by GitHub

The severity of consequences depends on the nature and severity of the violation.

This Code of Conduct applies to:
